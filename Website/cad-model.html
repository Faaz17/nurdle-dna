NurdleDNA

This presents the Nurdle DNA prototype design. The model is shown as a clean engineering view for reviewing the device body, sensing layout, containment pathway, and evidence-capture method.

Primary function
Inline prototype housing for monitoring industrial drains and pellet-transfer zones before leakage reaches stormwater or coastal discharge.
Detection architecture
AI vision identifies nurdles while VOC, turbidity, and flow readings verify the incident and classify its severity.
Response architecture
Confirmed leakage closes the valve and diverts contaminated flow into a removable evidence cartridge.
Evidence architecture
Captured pellets are retained for mass logging, proof imaging, cartridge removal, and auditable compliance reporting.
Operational method
The device works as a smart environmental system: detect, contain, preserve evidence, and generate a traceable incident record.
