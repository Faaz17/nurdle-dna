Introducing NurdleDNA

Industry is boxed in by the limits of traditional drain monitoring

Manual inspection is slow, sample-based testing is reactive, and pellet leakage often only gets noticed after it's already in the watershed. Conventional drains carry industrial waste straight to stormwater systems, soil, and the sea.

NurdleDNA is an inline IoT unit that detects, contains, weighs, and reports nurdle contamination in real time — at the source, before pollution escapes.

Architecture

Architected to deliver sub-second detection and containment, end-to-end — from optical flow cell to Firebase audit packet.

Detection unit

NVIDIA Jetson Nano runs YOLOv8n on the optical-flow-cell camera at 30 fps. MQ-135 gas + LDR turbidity probes corroborate the event before the FSM commits, eliminating false positives.

CAM

Containment unit

Arduino Uno runs the 5-state Moore FSM and drives the MG996R servo pinch valve. Closure within one second of S3 latch — flow diverts into the evidence cartridge instead of the drain.

FSM

Evidence unit

HX711 load cell weighs the captured cartridge in real time. UV LED 365 nm fluorescence excites pellet polymer, classified by AI into a forensic DNA Source field on every audit packet.

EVD

Performance

Higher detection capacity: AI vision flags 126 pellets per frame with 94% confidence on the optical flow cell. Multi-channel sensing eliminates false positives before the FSM ever commits.

Speed

Sub-second response: the servo pinch valve closes in under one second of an S3 latch. Sensor sample rate runs at 5 Hz, and alarm-to-actuation latency stays bounded.

Evidence

Audit-grade compliance: every event is timestamped, weighed by HX711 load cell, and written to Firebase with bay ID, density index, gas ppm, and DNA source — searchable, exportable, traceable.

Interoperability

Compatible with industry-standard IIoT platforms and existing drain infrastructure

NurdleDNA bolts inline to existing drain stacks. Power over USB-C, Ethernet for networking, and the Firebase audit packet pushes to any compliance dashboard.

Our software stack, arc-tech v0.4.0, abstracts hardware behind a JSON serial protocol. Operators see one dashboard. Engineers can swap sensors without touching the FSM.

The hardware stack

Two processors. Three sensor channels. One pinch valve. A pellet cartridge with a load cell. Every component below is wired into the FSM and observable in the live demo.

AI

NVIDIA Jetson Nano

AI vision & cloud uplink

Runs YOLOv8n on the optical-flow-cell camera feed, classifies pellets, computes the Density Index, and pushes the audit packet to Firebase Realtime Database.

  • 4 GB · ARM Cortex-A57
  • USB Serial → Arduino
μC

Arduino Uno

Real-time FSM & actuation

Reads analog sensors at 5 Hz, runs the 5-state Moore FSM, drives the servo pinch valve, and latches the alarm until the operator presses the physical RST button.

  • ATmega328P @ 16 MHz
  • I²C LCD, GPIO LEDs + buzzer
📷

CSI/USB Camera + UV LED

Optical detection

Captures the transparent flow-cell at 30 fps. A 365 nm UV LED ring excites pellet fluorescence so the AI can fingerprint the polymer source (DNA Source field).

  • 1080p · 30 fps
  • UV 365 nm
⚗

MQ-135 / MQ-4 Gas Sensor

Headspace VOC monitoring

Mounted in the chamber headspace. Detects volatile organic compounds and ammonia leakage from the pellet stream. Critical threshold latches the FSM into S3 (ALRM).

  • Analog → A1
  • Trip > 200 ppm
  • MQ-135 or MQ-4
◈

LDR Turbidity Probe

Water clarity proxy

Photoresistor pair across the flow cell. Reads water turbidity as a corroborating signal to the AI vision count — confirms a contamination event before the FSM commits.

  • Analog → A0
  • Trip > 600 raw
⚖

HX711 + Load Cell

Captured-mass evidence

Weighs the evidence cartridge during latched alarm. Reports captured mass in grams to the audit packet — physical proof for compliance reports.

  • 1–5 kg load cell
  • 24-bit ADC bridge
⏚

MG996R Servo + Pinch Valve

Containment actuator

180° servo squeezes a silicone tube to close the outlet path within one second of S3 latch — diverting contaminated flow into the cartridge instead of the drain.

  • 11 kg·cm torque
  • Closure time < 1 s
↻

Peristaltic Pump

Closed-loop flow

Maintains a constant water-flow rate through the optical cell so the sample is representative. Cuts off automatically on alarm to stop further contaminant egress.

  • 12 V DC · ~1.5 L/min
  • Closed-loop circulation
∿

Mixing Chamber

Anti-settling pre-stage

Inline turbulence section that homogenises the water sample before it enters the optical flow cell. Prevents pellets from settling so the AI sees a representative sample, not a stratified one.

  • Static mixing baffles
  • Upstream of camera
▣

LCD · LEDs · Buzzer

Local indicators

I²C 16×2 LCD displays the current FSM state and operator messages. RGB LEDs (green / yellow / red) and a piezo buzzer mirror the FSM output stage so a worker can read the status from across the bay.

  • I²C 0x27
  • GPIO 5 / 6 / 7 / 8
