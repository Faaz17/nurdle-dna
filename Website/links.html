NurdleDNA

"Watch every stream, all the time."

NurdleDNA is an inline IoT device that detects microplastic “nurdle” pellets in oil pipelines and industrial water in about 3 seconds — automatically closing a valve to stop the flow, capturing physical evidence, and logging an immutable audit trail to the cloud. Built on a dual-processor design (NVIDIA Jetson Nano AI vision + Arduino safety FSM), it’s the first sub-$250 inline system of its kind, bringing affordable, real-time microplastic monitoring to the UAE’s petrochemical industry.
